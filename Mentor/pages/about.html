About Skilliant

Transforming the informal labour market through technology, verification, and fair wages.

Our Mission

Empowering Skilled Labourers & Simplifying Hiring

Skilliant was founded to bridge the gap between skilled tradespeople looking for consistent work and homeowners/contractors seeking reliable, verified experts.

Every worker on our platform undergoes background verification, skill assessments, and customer review tracking to maintain high service quality.

Our Core Pillars

  • ⚡ Verified Identity: Government ID checks for safety.
  • 💧 Transparent Pricing: No hidden costs or sudden surges.
  • 🔨 Direct Escrow Payments: Fair payouts released upon customer satisfaction.
  • 🎨 Contractor Roster Management: Enabling teams to scale seamlessly.
