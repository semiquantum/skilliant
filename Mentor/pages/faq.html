Help Center & FAQ

Answers to common questions about bookings, payments, and worker safety.

How are labourers verified on Skilliant? ▼
Every skilled worker must submit government ID proof (Aadhaar / PAN), criminal background checks, and trade skill certifications before accepting jobs.
What payment methods are supported? ▼
We support Skilliant In-App Escrow Wallet, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, and Cash on Completion.
What happens if a worker cancels a booking? ▼
If a worker cancels, your wallet is instantly refunded 100% of the funds, and our dispatch system auto-assigns an alternative top-rated worker nearby.
Can contractors hire multiple labourers for a site? ▼
Yes! Contractor accounts can manage team rosters, assign multiple labourers to single commercial sites, and receive unified GST invoices.
