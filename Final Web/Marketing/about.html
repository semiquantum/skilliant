Mission & Vision

Empowering Skilled Hands, Protecting Every Customer

For decades, the skilled trades have been plagued by middlemen, predatory referral commissions, uncertain licensing, and inconsistent pricing.

Skilliant builds technology that gives master craftspeople direct ownership of their business, reviews, and client relationships while providing customers with verified backgrounds, transparent hourly pricing, and full escrow protection.

Our Mission

To make skilled trade services instantly accessible, transparently priced, and universally reliable for all.

Our Vision

To be the world’s most trusted labour marketplace where craft and customer dignity are always prioritized.

Guiding Principles

The Values That Drive Us

Every feature we build and every policy we enact is guided by four foundational commitments.

Uncompromising Trust

We verify licenses, criminal histories, and authentic references before any worker appears in search results.

Radical Transparency

No mystery fees, no opaque algorithms. Workers set their own honest rates and customers see exact prices upfront.

Dignity of Craft

We celebrate skilled vocational labor as essential engineering work, ensuring contractors receive fair compensation.

Speed & Reliability

From instant emergency water leaks to scheduled renovations, we connect you with active local pros within minutes.
