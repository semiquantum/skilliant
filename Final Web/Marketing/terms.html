Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: August 21, 2026. Please review the rules governing your use of the Skilliant marketplace platform.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account, browsing profiles, submitting booking requests, or offering skilled labour services through Skilliant ("the Platform"), you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and our Privacy Policy.

2. Platform Marketplace Role

Skilliant functions as a technology platform connecting independent trade professionals ("Workers") with property owners and project managers ("Customers"). Skilliant is not an employer, general contractor, or agent of individual workers. Workers operate as independent contractors setting their own rates and task scopes.

3. Worker Licensing & Standards

Workers offering trade services that legally mandate municipal or state licensing (e.g., master electrical, licensed plumbing, structural HVAC) warrant that their licenses and insurance policies are active, valid, and in good standing. Skilliant conducts verification audits but users are encouraged to review local permits.

4. Escrow Payments & Billing

To ensure complete trust and security, all transactions are processed through our authorized escrow architecture. Funds are pre-authorized upon booking and only disbursed to the worker once the customer explicitly approves the finished project or after the 48-hour post-completion review window.

5. Cancellations & Rescheduling

Customers may cancel or reschedule a scheduled booking with full refund up to 4 hours prior to the scheduled arrival time. Cancellations within 4 hours may be subject to a standard 1-hour dispatch trip fee to compensate the worker for travel time.

6. Prohibited Activities

Users may not: (a) bypass platform escrow for off-platform cash evasion, (b) post fraudulent or coerced reviews, (c) harass, intimidate, or discriminate against other users, or (d) misrepresent professional trade licenses or qualifications.

7. Limitation of Liability

While Skilliant provides up to $10,000 in escrow dispute protection on verified bookings, Skilliant is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential property damages resulting from independent contractor workmanship beyond our established guarantee policy.
