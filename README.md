# 🚀 Skilliant Admin Portal

A modern and responsive administrative management portal for **Skilliant**, designed to provide centralized control over users, labourers, contractors, bookings, payments, reports, analytics, activities, and platform settings.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🏠 **Dashboard** — Platform KPIs, financial overview, charts, and activity feed
- 👥 **User Management** — Manage users, profiles, status, and account actions
- 👷 **Labour Management** — Manage labourers, skills, availability, and records
- 🏢 **Contractor Management** — Manage company details, contacts, locations, and verification
- 🗂️ **Categories & Skills** — Manage platform categories and professional skills
- 📅 **Booking Management** — Create, view, edit, delete, search, filter, and manage bookings
- 💳 **Payments & Escrow** — Manage payments, escrow status, commissions, payouts, and refunds
- 📊 **Reports & Analytics** — Revenue, booking performance, user growth, and platform insights
- 📥 **Data Export** — Export selected report data
- 🔔 **Activity & Notifications** — Centralized administrative activities and notifications
- ⚙️ **Settings** — Profile, password, appearance, and platform configuration
- 👤 **Admin & Role Management** — Super Admin and Admin access management
- 🔍 **Search & Filtering** — Consistent search and filtering across modules
- 🪟 **Interactive Modals** — Add, view, edit, verify, confirm, and delete workflows
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Optimized for desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile

## 💰 Management Flow

**Booking → Payment → Wallet / Escrow → Revenue → Reports → Analytics → Export**

The financial and analytics sections are designed around connected platform data so that operational changes can be reflected in related reports and statistics.

## 🔐 Role Management

### Super Admin

Full access to platform management, administrator management, roles, settings, financial modules, and operational controls.

### Admin

Access is controlled according to the permissions assigned by the Super Admin.
