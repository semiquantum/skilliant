Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026. Your privacy and the confidentiality of your data are fundamental to the trust we build.

1. Introduction & Scope

Skilliant Inc. ("Skilliant", "we", "our", or "us") operates the online labour discovery and contractor connection platform available via our websites and applications. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect from registered homeowners, business customers, and skilled trade professionals ("Workers"), and explains how that data is used, stored, protected, and shared.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, email address, phone number, physical address or service area, profile photo, and password credentials.
  • Worker Verification Credentials: Trade license numbers, government-issued identification cards, liability insurance certificates, and background verification records.
  • Transaction & Booking Details: Service dates, hourly rates, project scope notes, payment authorization tokens (processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant gateways).
  • Device & Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, interaction logs, and cookie identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Facilitating search matching between customers and nearby qualified trade workers.
  • Conducting mandatory identity and criminal background screenings to uphold marketplace safety.
  • Processing secure escrow authorizations, milestone releases, and worker disbursements.
  • Preventing fraud, unauthorized account access, and policy violations.
  • Delivering important transactional SMS and email notifications regarding job requests.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosures

Skilliant does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to advertisers. We share data only with verified background screening providers, payment processing networks, cloud infrastructure hosts (AWS/GCP), and when legally mandated by statutory authorities or court orders.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All data in transit between your browser and our servers is secured using modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Sensitive information (such as tax IDs, background records, and payment tokens) is encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards with strict role-based access control.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We utilize essential and functional cookies to maintain your login session, save your dark/light theme preference, and preserve filter states. You may manage cookie settings through your browser preferences at any time.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections, demand deletion ("right to be forgotten"), or request an export of your transaction history. To exercise these rights, submit a request to privacy@skilliant.com.

If you have questions or concerns regarding our privacy standards, please reach out directly:
