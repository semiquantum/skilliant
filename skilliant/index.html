How do I add labour?

Open Labour from the sidebar and select Add Labour. Complete the required details and save the record.

How do I edit a labour record?

Open Labour, choose the edit action for the required worker, update the information and save it.

Where can I view payments?

Open Wallet to review payments, transactions and invoices.

Where are company documents?
